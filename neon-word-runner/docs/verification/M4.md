# M4 verification — world answer gates

Date: 2026-10-04

The fixed question panel was replaced by a street sign and three neon answer gates rendered by Phaser in a slow reading-parallax layer. Every gate shows its A/S/D, arrow, and 1/2/3 input hint; the same sign is clickable. The player remains visible below the signs while the route continues to scroll.
