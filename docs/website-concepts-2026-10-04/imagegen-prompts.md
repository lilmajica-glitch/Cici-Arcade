# CiciArcade 首页渲染提示词

模式：内置 Image Gen；用途：ui-mockup；日期：2026-10-04。

每次调用均实际附带用户 Logo 与现有首页截图。目标尺寸 1440 × 1920；真实返回尺寸以结果文件为准。

## 午夜街机

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create a realistic, production-quality redesign of the Chinese educational arcade website CiciArcade. This is a SINGLE complete homepage, not a mood board, not a comparison sheet. Target canvas 1440 pixels wide by 1920 pixels high (vertical 3:4), a naturally scrollable desktop website shown straight on, filling the entire image edge to edge. No browser chrome, monitor, device frame, environment, watermark, option numbers, concept title, or annotation.
Input images: Image 1 is the USER'S ORIGINAL BRAND LOGO. Retain its unmistakable glossy blue C-shaped mascot, navy eyes, peach cheeks and three tiny coral/yellow/mint marks, and the correctly spelled name "CiciArcade". Use the blue C mascot cleanly integrated on the website with its cream source background removed. Do not invent a different character, add limbs or accessories, or change the mascot's identity. Image 2 is the EXISTING WEBSITE, for content and real game-thumbnail grounding only; redesign its surrounding UI completely in the direction specified below. Reuse the laboratory-machine/doctor scene for the math game and the dark neon runner scene for the English game in polished contained thumbnails. Keep their game identities recognizable.
Product: A learning game platform with exactly TWO existing games: "Cici 小博士" (math music arcade) and "Neon Word Runner" (English city runner). Primary outcome: quickly choose and launch a game; supporting outcome: understand how end-of-round scores, mistakes and learning suggestions help the next round. Student-facing and welcoming to parents, sophisticated and accessible.
Design a focused homepage with clear hierarchy, strong typography, purposeful spacing, 72–88px page margins, readable 16px body copy, 24px game titles and 64–80px main headline in no more than TWO lines. Generous 100–150px breathing space between major chapters. Do not invent more features merely to fill space. No fake partners, testimonials, school logos, ratings, completion rates, user counts, leaderboards, subscriptions, login UI or analytics widgets.
Required complete page narrative: a premium minimal navigation with small original mascot + wordmark at left and "游戏大厅", "学习方式" and a clear primary blue/white CTA at right; a striking hero with exact main headline "把练习，" / "玩成冒险。" (only two lines), supporting copy "玩一局。懂一点。再来一局。", primary CTA "进入游戏大厅" and quiet secondary link "了解学习方式"; the curated games region headed "今天，玩点什么？" with the two actual game entries and launch affordances; a calm lower learning-feedback region headed "通关之后，还有下一步。" with concise labels "本局成绩", "错题回顾", "下一局建议" showing the idea of the real post-game feedback without fabricated numbers; a spacious final CTA and small footer with "保持好奇，继续开玩。" and "CiciArcade".
Game text must be correctly rendered, not gibberish: Math name "Cici 小博士", subtitle "疯狂博士大作战", meta "20 道加减法 · 不限时", button "开始游戏". English name "Neon Word Runner", subtitle "霓虹单词跑酷", meta "12 道单词题 · 85 秒一局", button "开始游戏".
Layout priority: spacing, grouping, alignment and typography first; lightweight row dividers second; subtle tint third. Use cards only for the two game objects, never card-inside-card. A game grid must contain exactly two purposeful entries, fully occupying its two columns with no blank cell. Keep the rest of the page on the base canvas. Include the learning region and footer in the image, with no unintended clipping or warping.
Chinese-first UI, crisp professional Chinese sans-serif paired with one Latin display family. No Inter. Carefully typeset readable Simplified Chinese. Main title is wide and readable. Buttons have perfect contrast. No emojis, rainbow backgrounds, kindergarten pastel palette, bubble comic text, floating stickers/badges, arbitrary hero statistics, spam tags, giant toy-like UI, cheap "SECTION 01" labels, stock photography or wall of controls.
These are visual mockups; show a clean plausible frozen frame of the proposed motion, not arrows or animation annotations. Current user-local date is 2026-10-04; avoid dates/time-sensitive mock data.
ART DIRECTION — MIDNIGHT ATELIER:
Build a rich ink-navy and sapphire premium arcade homepage. Background #0b1323 / #101d31, cool off-white #eff5fc type, restrained electric brand blue #1886ff. Avoid purple cyberpunk gradients and turquoise dashboard aesthetics. Subtle material grain, soft blue directional illumination, crisp thin dividers and deep sophisticated contrast.
Hero architecture is ARTISTIC ASYMMETRY: broad two-line Chinese headline offset toward upper left with generous negative space; the exact glossy C mascot is a large sculptural object overlapping the lower right of the hero. Its recognizable blue silhouette is softly edge-lit, sitting on a subtle circular midnight stage; there is no white square behind it. CTAs sit directly below the left text. Do not use a standard equal left/right split. Outfit-inspired Latin typography and strong elegant Chinese sans, no comic font.
Games are displayed as an expanding horizontal two-panel gallery across the page: one slightly wider math scene, one slightly narrower neon scene, both fully readable with title/metadata/CTA outside the artwork. Moody dark mat surrounding the colorful actual game thumbnails. Use sophisticated 14–18px panel radii, sparing blue glow on the active CTA, no heavy outline boxes elsewhere.
Use a narrow quiet typographic PLAY / LEARN / REPLAY movement line as a transition in the lower page, not a fake partner logo row. A small photographic-style game-art insert may sit inline in the lower learning section title, never obstructing the hero title. Learning feedback reads as a calm editorial sequence on the same dark base canvas, using three concise labels and ample whitespace.
Planned motion mood: the mascot and artwork grow slightly into view, then dim smoothly on scroll; supporting learning copy reveals progressively. Render a pristine still state of this flow. Strong luminous brand identity, immersive arcade atmosphere and clearly usable educational product.
```
